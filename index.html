About Us

A trusted Nigerian developer with a global outlook.

Muntasrab Global Concept Ltd is a registered Nigerian limited liability company working in real estate development and construction.

We believe good property starts with clear title, sound engineering and honest communication. We take care of each stage for our clients, from sourcing land and getting approvals to building and finishing, so owners and investors can buy with confidence.

What We Do

Property services under one roof

Real Estate Development

Planning and delivery of residential estates, housing units and mixed-use developments.

Building Construction

Land Acquisition & Sales
