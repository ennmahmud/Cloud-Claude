About Us

An indigenous engineering firm with a global outlook.

Muntasrab Global Concept Ltd is a registered Nigerian limited liability company offering civil engineering, building construction and consultancy services.

Our engineers, surveyors and project managers work together from design and costing through construction and handover. We build infrastructure that lasts and that serves the communities using it.

Leadership

Engr. Muntari Sagir Malumfashi, FNICE

Chief Executive

Engr. Muntari Sagir Malumfashi leads Muntasrab Global Concept Ltd. In October 2025 he was made a Fellow of the Nigerian Institution of Civil Engineers (FNICE) at the Institution's 23rd annual conference in Kano.

Outside the company, he has personally funded healthcare, education and infrastructure projects in his home community of Malumfashi, Katsina State. Governor Dikko Umaru Radda commissioned these projects in April 2026.

Community Impact

Giving back to Malumfashi

On 20 April 2026, Katsina State Governor Malam Dikko Umaru Radda commissioned community projects funded personally by our Chief Executive in Malumfashi Local Government Area.

  • Al-Makiyayi Healthcare CentreA new, fully equipped healthcare centre for the Al-Makiyayi / Danjanku community.
  • Surgical theatre, Tsamiya HospitalA modern operating theatre in Malumfashi.
  • Classrooms, Nana Babajo College of MidwiferyA block of three classrooms with an office.
  • Road & 1 km drainage systemAround the Sambo Primary School area of Malumfashi town.
  • Perimeter fencingSambo Cemetery and Sambo Primary School.
  • School vehicleDonated to Government Girls' Science Secondary School, Malumfashi.
"If more individuals emulate this kind of gesture, the burden on government in providing infrastructure and social services will be significantly reduced." Malam Dikko Umaru Radda, Governor of Katsina State, 20 April 2026

Source: Katsina State Government press release.
