## 3. Community projects commissioned 20 April 2026, Malumfashi LGA, Katsina State

Commissioned by Governor Dikko Umaru Radda. Both sources say they were paid for
by Engr. Muntari Sagir **personally** ("from proceeds of his business
ventures" / "with his personal funds as … sadaqatul jariya"). They are the
CEO's philanthropy, not Muntasrab contracts. Describe them that way on the site.

Also in the government release:
- Dr. Shamsuddeen Yahya (Executive Secretary, State Primary Health Care
  Development Agency): three years of contributions, "the establishment of
  laboratories, maternity wards, eye care units, and the upgrading of several
  healthcare centres."
