## About Engr. Muntari Sagir Malumfashi (not confirmed as linked to the company)

The Katsina State Government reported on 20 April 2026 that Governor Radda
commissioned projects paid for personally by Engr. Muntari Sagir "from proceeds
of his business ventures":

- 1 km drainage system in Malumfashi town
- Perimeter fence completed at Sambo Cemetery
- Fence at Sambo Primary School
- Block of three classrooms with an office at Nana Babajo College of Midwifery, Malumfashi
- Healthcare centre at Al-Makiyayi
- Pledge: perimeter fence for the graveyard behind Aliyu Primary School
- The State Primary Health Care Development Agency (Dr. Shamsuddeen Yahya) cited three years of his contributions to the health sector

Source: [Katsina State Government](https://katsinastate.gov.ng/2026/04/20/governor-radda-urges-affluent-individuals-to-support-community-development/),
also reported by [Dateline Nigeria](https://dateline.ng/governor-radda-meets-apc-female-aspirants-in-katsina-2/).
Only the search listings were readable here; the pages themselves were blocked.

**Not found anywhere:** that he is the MD/CEO of Muntasrab, or that Muntasrab
built these projects. The article presents them as his personal philanthropy.
